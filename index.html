How quoting works

Real timings, not guesses

I time my own painting sessions stage by stage, so quotes come from data rather than a rough guess. Each quote covers the painting, a buffer, and the time for progress updates, messages and careful packing.

You get one flat price before anything starts.

  1. Enquire. Tell me what you want painted and which tier.

Questions

FAQ

Do you paint full armies?

Yes, at box-art standard. Silver tier is designed for armies: the armour and main surfaces get the full Gold treatment, with lighter work on small details.

Why is there no price list?

The colour scheme, the models and the tier all change the hours. Each project is quoted individually from real timing data, so the price is fair for the actual job.

When can I book?

Slots open in January 2027. Join the waitlist and you'll hear first, with no obligation and no deposit to join.

Where are you based?

Inverness, in the Scottish Highlands.
